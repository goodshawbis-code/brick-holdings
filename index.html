About

One holding company. Distinct ventures.

Emet Holdings owns and operates a focused portfolio of AI-powered applications, each built for a specific community and each with its own identity.

We build software that gives people real tools and real ownership: artists who keep control of their music, athletes who train like professionals, contractors who bid and build with precision, and legal professionals who work with greater clarity. Every venture stands on its own. Together, they share a single standard.
