Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I upload a blueprint? A: Tap the Blueprints tab inside your project, then tap the + button to upload a PDF from your device.
Q: How do I place a pin on a blueprint? A: Open a blueprint, then tap anywhere on the PDF to drop a pin. Add a note and select pin type (Issue, Markup, or Question).
Q: How do I order materials from a distributor? A: From the Estimate tab, tap "Order Now" on an approved materials request, select your distributor, confirm the line items, and the app will send the purchase order.
Q: Can I add a new distributor? A: Yes. Tap the + button in the Distributors list to submit a new distributor for approval.
Q: How do I export an estimate? A: In the Estimate tab, tap the Export button to generate a shareable summary.
