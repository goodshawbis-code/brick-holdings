Privacy Policy – Plum Level and Square

Last Updated: September 6, 2026

Brick Outdoor Living, Inc. ("us", "we", or "our") operates the Plum Level and Square mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you:

  • Email Address – Used for magic link authentication to securely access your account.
  • Project Data – Construction project names, addresses, and client names entered within the app.
  • Blueprint Files – PDF documents uploaded by users, stored securely in encrypted cloud storage.
  • Estimate and Materials Data – Line-item estimates, quantities, and materials information you create within the app.
  • Location-Tagged Pins – Job site annotations and pin data placed on blueprints.
  • Camera / Photo Library Access – Used for uploading blueprint documents and job site photos.
  • Usage Data – Information about how the Service is accessed and used, such as device type, operating system version, and app interaction data.

How We Use Your Information

Brick Outdoor Living, Inc. uses the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To provide customer support
  • To gather analysis and improve the Service
  • To monitor the usage of the Service
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely via Supabase. Blueprint files are stored in encrypted cloud storage. We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
