A New Sport

SlamboX is a high-speed, original sport played inside racquetball-style courts with 8-foot basketball goals. No out of bounds. Every wall is in play. Slam dunks, alley-oops, and bank shots off any surface — all with a dodgeball-style basketball designed for grip and power.

Built for kids and adults as a fast-paced basketball alternative. Competitive 2v2 or 3v3 league-style format where showmanship is rewarded.

How It Works

The Court

Racquetball-style enclosed rooms with four walls, a ceiling, and an 8-foot basketball hoop. No sidelines, no out of bounds — play off every surface.

The Ball

A custom dodgeball-style basketball engineered for grip, bounce, and aggressive dunking. Light enough to throw hard, grippy enough to palm one-handed.

The Teams

2v2 or 3v3 competitive squads. League-style format with rankings, playoffs, and championships. Every player touches the ball, every second counts.

No Boundaries

Walls are your teammate. Bank a pass off the ceiling, alley-oop off the back wall, or slam a putback off the side. Creativity is the meta.

The Official SlamboX Ball

Engineered for maximum grip and explosive bounce inside enclosed courts. Dodgeball DNA meets basketball soul — designed to be palmed, thrown, and slammed through an 8-foot rim at full speed.

Scoring System

Points are based on difficulty and showmanship. Style wins games.

PLAY DESCRIPTION PTS
Standard Bucket Clean shot through the hoop 1
Wall Bank Shot Ball banks off any wall before going in 2
Slam Dunk Above-the-rim finish on the 8-foot goal 2
Alley-Oop Pass to aerial finish — direct or off wall 3
Ceiling Bank Ball touches ceiling before scoring 3
Showtime Play Multi-wall combo or behind-the-back finish (ref judged) 4
