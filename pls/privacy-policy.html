This policy describes Plumb Level and Square ("PLS"), an iOS construction bidding and project coordination app published by PLS Software LLC. PLS Software LLC is a separate business owned by Emet Group LLC.

Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you use and the information you provide, PLS may handle account and sign-in information; company, membership, role, and invitation records; project, job, bid, and estimate information; documents or other content you add; support messages; and subscription status or transaction references needed to provide subscription features.

Cloud storage and team sharing

PLS uses Superapp Cloud to store and synchronize app records. People authorized by a company may see shared company and project information according to their access and role. Only invite people authorized to receive that information.

Optional Gmail bid intake

If you choose to connect Gmail for bid intake, PLS uses the Google access you authorize to process bid-invitation email and related attachments for that feature. PLS does not use Google user data for advertising or sell it. PLS's access to Google user data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access in your Google Account. Revocation stops future access through that authorization; it does not by itself establish what happens to information already copied into PLS.

How information is used and shared

PLS uses information to provide app features, synchronize records, support company collaboration, perform bid-intake actions you request, administer subscriptions, protect the service, respond to support, and meet legal obligations. Company information is shared with members the company authorizes. Service providers process information as needed to provide the app and connected features.

Retention and account deletion

Pending verification: PLS has not yet confirmed the retention periods for account, company, project, bid, estimate, connected-email, support, billing, or backup data; the effects of deleting one member's account on shared company records; or the time needed to complete deletion. Do not assume that uninstalling the app deletes an account or cloud-stored information. See the Account Deletion page for the current status of verified instructions.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed monthly. The subscription area is on the Office side of PLS under the Settings gear. The billing provider, renewal behavior, cancellation steps, refund terms, and whether account deletion stops billing are still being confirmed. Do not assume account deletion cancels a subscription.

Children

PLS is a business construction tool and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through PLS, the public support contact is still being established.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when PLS's features or data practices change. PLS does not yet have a verified, active company support email. The planned address support@pls.emetholdings.co has not been activated; do not use it to submit requests or sensitive information. See the Support page for current status.
